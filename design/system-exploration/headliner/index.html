Plursky design systems · Direction 03 · Poster

Headliner

The lineup poster is the culture's most-screenshotted artifact. Ravers zoom in, circle names and argue about the font size. HEADLINER makes the app that poster. Type is the image, and every night prints in its own two riso inks. Your plan gets billed like a headliner, so the night you built becomes a poster you can post. The ground is deep ultramarine, not black, so it still reads at 3 AM.

  • Type is the image
  • Two inks per night
  • You set the billing
  • Print, not physics
  • Photos get printed, not pasted
Signature: your posterLineup has a Poster view. Your picks are billed top-tier in pink, and the rest sits below by stage. It's the flyer with you as the promoter.
Signature: the night's print runFriday prints black and orange, Saturday blue and pink, Sunday green and yellow. The app reprints itself each night, and a screenshot tells you which night it was.
Why ravers get itRiso flyers, misregistered ink and condensed billing type are the scene's own print culture, from warehouse flyers to today's lineup drops.
The honest costIt's the loudest of the three. A blue ground costs more OLED power than black, and it asks the most of type discipline across 13 festivals.
