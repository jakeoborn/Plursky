Holo

PLUR made tactile. The ground is cool pearl, never cream. The festival's collectibles become the interface: a holographic wristband, kandi letter beads, and a holo trading card for every set you catch. Foil appears only on things you've earned or are wearing, so it stays special. After sunset the app switches to Blacklight, where the same foil glows like UV paint. It's easy on the eyes at 3 AM and still unmistakably Plursky.
