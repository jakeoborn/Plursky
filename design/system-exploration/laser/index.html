Plursky design systems · Direction 01 · Dark

Laser

The interface draws in light the way a rig does. The ground is a quiet violet-black, and every colour is a laser wavelength with one job. Your night is rendered as beams, so the Today screen is a small light show made from the schedule itself. It stays calm to read at 3 AM, and it looks like nothing else on a raver's phone.

  • Colour is light, never paint
  • One lifted thing per screen: the glow
  • Tempo is the motion clock (128 BPM)
  • Strobe-safe by rule
  • Photos support, never lead
Signature: the rigToday's hero is a fan of nine beams, one per stage, fired from the stage floor. A stage with a set on your plan fires white-hot; a live stage glows faintly; an empty one stays dark. It's the schedule, drawn as a light show.
Signature: the light trailMe turns every set you actually saw into one long-exposure stroke, coloured by the hour. It's a poster only your night could make, which is exactly what people share.
Why ravers get itLasers, the Eurostile flyer caps and a 128 BPM pulse are native to the room. Nothing here is borrowed from a ticketing app.
Why it stays calmA near-black violet ground, thin marks and one glow per screen. Spectrum colours cover less than 5% of any screen.
